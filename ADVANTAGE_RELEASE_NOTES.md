# U SPORTS — 2026-only provisional predictions

No held-out accuracy or error-band claim is made for this small sample. No older-season coefficients, player data, market lines or saved predictions are reused.

Teams without enough verified 2026 history remain unavailable. Live scores and gamebooks remain functional; fitted live probabilities and conditional winning thresholds are withheld until there is sufficient 2026-only training data. Older-season forecast snapshots were explicitly withdrawn.
